## 8. Known drift + refactor targets

> The canonical isolate drift registry. Siblings cite entries here BY NUMBER
> (e.g. [§5](05-5-the-isolate-case-format.md) item 3) — numbering is
> append-only: a new drift is appended as the next number, never inserted or
> renumbered. The status column tracks each drift through three states:
> **OPEN** (unresolved, no call inline), **DECIDED** (unresolved, but the
> fix is already settled — see the row's resolution owner), and
> **RESOLVED** (fixed, marked in place, never deleted).

> **[DECIDE]** Exact labels for the three-state status model above.
> Recommended default: `OPEN` / `DECIDED` / `RESOLVED` — cleanly
> distinguishes a drift still needing owner input from one that's already
> settled and just waiting to land, without adding a fourth state.
