## 4. Structural tensions a refactor should resolve

A **structural tension** is a still-live design tension that spans ≥2
subsystems — distinct from [§1](01-1-hydration-architecture-pain-from-isolate-feedback-md-2026-.md)'s
per-bug fixes (each already FIXED on `develop`, pinned to one bug with a narrow
per-bug design lesson) and from
[DX-IDEAL §4](../DX-IDEAL/05-4-the-biggest-cross-cutting-forks.md)'s cross-cutting
fork inventory (the open product decisions themselves). Each row below routes
to its per-subsystem detail and its DX-IDEAL resolution or fork; read the row,
then follow both links — nothing is re-argued here.

| Tension | What it is | Status | Detail — per-subsystem home | DX-IDEAL resolution/fork |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **The one-shot hydration model** | Islands hydrate via one bootstrap scan plus rescue mechanisms (rescan, props bridge, eager selector registry) that must independently agree. | resolved-in-DX-IDEAL (clear win) | spec 03 [§10](../03-islands-and-hydration/10-10-contract-checklist-for-a-refactor.md) | [§3.3](../DX-IDEAL/04-3-per-subsystem-ideal.md) "One owned lifecycle state machine" / [§5 step 2](../DX-IDEAL/06-5-build-order-max-dx-leverage-first.md) |
| **Selector-keyed registries vs folder-path identity** | Component identity is the folder path, but several registries (`astFor`'s bare-selector lookup) still key by selector — a page and a page-local island sharing a basename can collide. | open fork | spec 02 [§7](../02-template-compiler/08-7-contract-checklist-for-a-refactor-each-pinned-by-a-named-t.md) item 9 / spec 03 [§1](../03-islands-and-hydration/01-1-the-island-model.md) | [§3.2](../DX-IDEAL/04-3-per-subsystem-ideal.md) "Component-tag resolution is loud on miss" closes the silent-miss/collision surface; carrying full folder-path identity end-to-end stays undecided |
